FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is MelodyPay free to join?

Yes. Creating an account is completely free. You start earning from your very first song.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Once your balance reaches ₦1,000, tap Withdraw on your dashboard, enter your bank details and the amount. Payouts are processed within 24 hours.

How much can I earn per day?

It depends on how much you listen and tap. Use the earnings estimator above to get an idea — active users earn more through daily check-ins and referrals.

How does the referral program work?

Share your unique referral link. When a friend signs up and completes their first task, you get ₦1,000 added to your wallet.
